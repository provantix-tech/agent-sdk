8. Method

  • A record belongs to the period of the UTC day it was ingested.
  • tokens_in is all input including the cached part; tokens_out is all output including reasoning.
  • Estimated savings are projections. Checked savings are read from the evidence after a window, per unit. The two are never added together.
  • By the resolved tag on each record (a tag on the record, then on the agent, then a person's department, then a rule). A call with no value is its own "(unallocated)" line and is never spread over the others.
  • Each model call is priced when it arrives, from the tokens the source reported and the price table row in effect that day: uncached input, cached read, cache write and output at their own prices, times any batch, priority or flex multiplier. A call with no price is counted and has no estimate.
  • Provantix's tokens and cost for each provider, model and UTC day against the provider's own figures, for days that have settled, within the tolerances below.

9. What this statement does not show

  • Anything outside AI and AI agents: this is not a statement of the organization's costs.
  • What the provider billed or what was paid. The provider's figures are what its own report said at the time they were fetched; an invoice is not read.
  • Spend by a provider Provantix is not connected to, or by a source that sent nothing, or calls that were never recorded: those are "untracked" or "not connected" and are named where known.
  • Whether any spend was necessary, correct or worth it, or that any call was authorised. Cost per outcome is a separate report and puts no value on an outcome.
  • A cause for any difference between Provantix's figures and a provider's.
  • That the figures are audited. It is cost evidence that supports an audit; it is not an audited financial statement.
